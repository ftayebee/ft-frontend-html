Case study

Nimbus Analytics

Turning a cluttered analytics suite into a focused, fast dashboard that teams actually look forward to opening.

Client
Northwind (sample)
Year
2025
Services
UX, UI, Dev
Stack
Vue, Node

Overview

Northwind's analytics product had grown feature-first for three years. Power users tolerated it; everyone else bounced. The brief: keep the depth, lose the friction.

The challenge

Seventeen navigation items, four competing chart styles, and a dashboard that took eight seconds to become interactive. New users couldn't find their first insight.

Research

I ran five usability sessions and mapped the most common tasks. A clear pattern emerged: 80% of sessions touched just three of the seventeen areas. That insight reshaped the whole IA.

Strategy

We collapsed navigation to a focused core, introduced a single consistent chart language, and committed to a 2-second time-to-interactive budget as a non-negotiable.

Design solution

Development

The frontend was rebuilt as a component-driven SPA with code-splitting and a shared design system. A slimmer API and aggressive caching brought time-to-interactive under two seconds.
