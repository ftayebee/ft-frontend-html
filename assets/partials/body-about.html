My story

From curious tinkerer to trusted partner.

I started building websites as a teenager, taking apart templates to understand how they worked. That curiosity turned into a craft — and then a career spanning startups, agencies and independent clients across three continents.

Today I work at the intersection of design and engineering. I believe the best products come from people who can both imagine an interface and build it well, without losing the detail in hand-off. That's the value I bring.
